## 2) One-Paragraph Pitch

Hugga is a curated hospitality layer that helps Airbnb hosts offer trusted local breakfast through vetted providers. Guests add breakfast for €15 per person per night, providers receive €11, and Hugga keeps €4 as the marketplace agent fee. Hosts earn Hugga Credits, not cash, so incentives stay aligned with guest experience. The system is reputation-first: reliable providers get priority access while assignment remains fair through controlled randomness. The result is simple—better mornings for guests, calmer operations for hosts, and predictable demand for providers.

## 3) 30-Second Pitch Script

“Most short-stay guests want a better morning, but hosts don’t want food logistics chaos. Our assignment model is reputation-weighted and fair, rewarding reliability without creating monopolies. It’s not delivery, and it’s not a marketplace free-for-all—it’s structured hospitality.”

## 16) Visual Direction Guidelines (For Design Handoff)

### Brand mood
- Calm, premium, human.
- Structured layouts with generous whitespace.
- Warmth through imagery, not visual clutter.

### Color direction
- Base: soft off-white / warm ivory background.
- Accent neutrals: stone, sand, muted olive, terracotta hints.
- Text: deep charcoal (not pure black) for softer contrast.

### Typography
- Modern sans-serif family.
- Medium-weight headlines, regular body text.
- Tight headline tracking, comfortable body line-height.
- Clear type scale with strong visual hierarchy.

### Spacing and layout
- Generous vertical rhythm (80–120px section spacing on desktop).
- Centered content blocks with restrained max-width.
- Card components with subtle borders, minimal shadows.
- Avoid dense multi-column clutter.

### Imagery style
- Natural morning light.
- Real kitchens, breakfast textures, host-home context.
- Local people, authentic craft moments.
- Avoid glossy stock clichés and overly staged business scenes.

### UI style principles
- Minimal nav, clear CTA hierarchy.
- One primary action per section.
- Quiet transitions and understated iconography.
- Trust signals integrated contextually, not as loud badges.
